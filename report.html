What helps investigators

Be specific

Dates, times, exact addresses or intersections, vehicle descriptions, and what you observed — not just “something felt off.”

Tip quality

Photos & video

SafeTampa / tip411 can accept media. Do not confront suspects — document from a safe place.

Evidence

City issues ≠ crime tips

Potholes, streetlights, and code issues go to 311 or Code Enforcement — not anonymous crime tip lines.
