Neighborhood grants

Funding programs Heights-area associations and neighbors can use — from Hillsborough mini-grants to tree planting, cleanups, and Tampa CRA support.

For Ybor Heights: The Neighborhood Association can apply when listed with Hillsborough County Neighborhood Relations. Nearby V.M. Ybor recently used a county mini-grant for the Untold Stories documentary and Cuscaden walking tour — a model for Heights projects. Contact neighborhood-relations@hcfl.gov · (813) 272-5860.

Hillsborough Tree Grant

Matching grant for neighborhood associations to plant trees and drought-tolerant plants in community areas or right-of-ways. Typically once every two years per association.

hcfl.gov · Tree Grant

Community Cleanup Program

County roll-off dumpsters for neighborhood cleanups (primarily unincorporated Hillsborough). City of Tampa neighborhoods should also ask Solid Waste / Keep Tampa Bay Beautiful for city options.

hcfl.gov · Cleanup

Tampa CRA Commercial Grants

Interior, exterior, and special-project grants for commercial properties inside CRA boundaries. Schedule a pre-app meeting: (813) 274-8325. Check whether your parcel falls in a Tampa CRA.
